07 — FAQ

WHY VALØR?

VALØR is a competitive RIVALS clan where you can meet skilled players, improve your mechanics, and participate in clan activities.
We want active, skilled and respectful players who enjoy competitive RIVALS and are willing to improve.
Players can progress from T5 → T4 → T3 → T2 → T1 → T∞, with T∞ being the highest legendary tier.
Knife gameplay is a major part of VALØR, but the clan is open to players who enjoy competitive RIVALS and want to contribute.
Join the VALØR Discord, read the clan information, and follow the current recruitment or tryout instructions.
